The captured Experimental Pi's image is NOT in Dropbox - it is 24 GB and would sync to the cloud.

    /home/mseifert/.local/share/piboy-backup-image/
        boot.pcl.zstd    39M   partclone.vfat  + zstd
        root.pcl.zstd    23G   partclone.ext4  + zstd
        MANIFEST.txt           sha256 sums (copy; the original is here in migration/)

Restore needs a target partition >= 939G. partition-table.sfdisk in this
directory reproduces the original layout - replay it verbatim rather than
inventing one, or partclone will refuse to restore into a smaller p2.

This is currently the ONLY copy of THIS PARTICULAR CARD - our unit, our ROMs, our
settings - and it lives on one laptop disk. That is not a backup. Copy it to
external media.
